About

Lu Guang · 陆光咖啡

Small-lot coffee, roasted in daylight and shipped the same week it's roasted.

What we do

We buy coffee in small lots, roast it on one machine, and sell it before it has time to get old. Nothing sits in a warehouse waiting for a season to turn.

The shelf changes as harvests land — Ethiopia, Colombia, Kenya, Peru, Burundi — and where a lot is good we go back to the same washing station the next year rather than chase something new.
