Brew guide

Pour-over, V60

A clean, repeatable recipe for a single cup. Anything in a cone works — the ratio matters more than the brewer.

V60 Espresso Iced

At a glance

Ratio
1:16
Dose
15 g
Water
240 g at 94 °C
Grind
medium-fine
Total time
2:45

Filtered water, 60–100 ppm. Rinse the paper before you start.

01

Rinse and dose

Rinse the filter with hot water, discard it, then add 15 g of coffee and level the bed.

02

Bloom, 45 g

Pour 45 g in a slow spiral and wait 40 seconds. The bed should rise and settle.

03

First pour, to 150 g

Pour steadily in circles, keeping the bed level. Avoid the paper wall.

04

Final pour, to 240 g

Finish by 1:45. Give the brewer one gentle swirl and let it draw down.

05

Draw down and taste

Clear by 2:45. Sour means grind finer; bitter and dry means coarser.
