Privacy

This website

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers, and loads nothing from other companies’ servers. Like any web host, ours keeps standard request logs, including IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running.

When you buy

Checkout is run by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details, and we never see your full card number.

From Stripe we receive your name, email address, shipping address and what you ordered. We use them to roast, pack and deliver your order and to reply if you contact us — nothing else. We don’t sell or share them.

Your data

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time: reply to the receipt Stripe emails you after checkout — it comes with our support address. We keep order records only as long as tax and accounting rules require.

If this changes, we’ll update this page. Last updated October 2026.
