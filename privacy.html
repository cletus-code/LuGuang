Privacy

We collect as little as we can: only what we need to hold coffee for you.

What we collect

When you reserve coffee, we receive your email address and the coffees on your wishlist, with their prices. That’s all: we don’t ask for your name, address or payment details, and there are no accounts.

What stays in your browser

Your wishlist and your choice of English or Chinese are saved in your own browser, so they’re there when you come back. They never leave your device, and we can’t see them. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use your email

We use your email only to hold your reservation and to write to you about it, including when the online shop opens. We won’t add you to a newsletter, and we never sell or share your email for marketing.

Who handles it

Two services help us run the site:

  • Formspree receives the reservation form and forwards it to our inbox. It keeps a copy of each reservation until we delete it.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website. Like any web host, it processes basic technical details, such as your IP address, to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks.

How long we keep it

We keep your reservation until it’s fulfilled or cancelled, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, reply to any email we’ve sent you, or ask us at the café. We’ll take care of it promptly.
