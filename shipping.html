Shipping & returns

When it ships

We roast every Monday and ship every Tuesday. Your order goes out on the first Tuesday after you place it, so the beans reach you days after roasting rather than weeks.

What it costs

Shipping is free on orders over $40. Below that, the shipping cost is shown at checkout, before you pay.

The countries we ship to are listed at checkout. If yours isn’t there, we can’t deliver to it yet.

Returns

Coffee is food, so we can’t take back opened bags. If an order arrives damaged, or isn’t what you ordered, tell us within 14 days of delivery and we’ll send a replacement or refund it in full.

Getting in touch

For anything about an order, reply to the receipt Stripe emails you after checkout — it comes with our support address.
